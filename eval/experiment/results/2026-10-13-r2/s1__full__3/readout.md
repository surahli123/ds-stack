## What would change the call

- A pre-exposure comparison showing the arms already differed on Successful Search Rate would mean the test is broken and the result cannot be trusted.
- A daily series by days since first exposure showing the lift fading toward zero in the last days would mean the effect is a novelty and needs more time.
- An owner correction to the 0.50% bar or the guardrail margins would reopen the call.

## Why it moved

- [measured] Successful Search Rate rose 0.81% with the Ranker v7 model as the only parameter that differs between arms.
- [guess] The segment cuts of Successful Search Rate (mobile, desktop, head, tail) show no clear segment carrying the lift, so the mechanism is not known.
