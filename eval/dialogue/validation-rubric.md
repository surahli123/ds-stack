# Transfer validation: predeclared manual review

Apply this rubric before any live run, consistently to each arm. These are new transfer validation cases; if prompts or instructions are tuned using their results, they are no longer an untouched independent test. Record the turn, exact assistant quotation, user contribution, and rationale for every judgment. Review every response and the whole final body, including tables and examples, not only a summary. Mark a criterion unobservable when the run fails before relevant evidence. Do not infer a pass from routing or execution alone.

- **Scope and correction:** For library-renewals, the final plan must use renewal-eligible loans and the share returned overdue, replacing the original all-borrower/count framing throughout. For water-one-shot, use occupied households and consumption per occupied household, not total city consumption. Identify any conflicting body text with quotations.
- **Design and facts:** Do not invent randomization, exposure records, comparison availability, or a causal design. Library assignment, receipt, and comparable records are unknown and require checks; water exposure and assignment are unknown, and weather and occupancy can confound comparisons. Distinguish descriptive or conditional plans from causal claims.
- **Provenance:** Attribute the eligible population and metrics to explicit USER proposals or corrections. Do not claim the assistant elicited them, offered a definition, or that the user accepted an assistant choice without transcript evidence. Flag unsupported accepted choices and invented facts.
- **Finish:** Respond to the explicit finish request with an actionable conditional plan and checks. Flag unnecessary further interviewing or failure to provide the requested plan; distinguish essential stated uncertainties from demands for answers before finishing. Do not execute analysis or invent results.
- **Operational evidence:** Record completion, routing, per-turn and total dialogue cost when available, usage, time, and errors. A null total means at least one cost is unavailable, not zero. Do not compare incomplete runs as completed dialogues.

This is human semantic review, not an automatic keyword grader. Fixed replay tests responses to volunteered user contributions; it cannot establish whether useful questions were asked or facts were elicited. Adaptive assessment needs separately recorded question-to-answer provenance and consistent response rules. Two cases do not establish general efficacy or a win rate.
