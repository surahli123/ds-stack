Yes, and the plan already accounts for it. That's why the segment is defined from each user's own pre-test share of sessions with a 3P result, not from `tenants.segment`. If I had labeled every user in the 61 "3P power" tenants as a 3P user, the segment would include many users who never see a 3P result. That would dilute any panel effect and could hide real harm or benefit among the users the PM cares about. The same label would also miss heavy 3P users in standard tenants. The plan already reports the overlap between the two definitions, with the tenant label kept as a secondary view only. One small addition: I'd also show how many users in "3P power" tenants fall below the behavior threshold, so the PM sees the size of the gap. That is a reporting detail, so the closing lines stay the same.

Unit: user (the randomized unit); per-user success rate over the test window, adjusted for tenant
Segment: a 3P user is a user whose own pre-test share of sessions with a 3P result (used_3p_result, 2026-06-01 to 08-31, minimum session count) is at or above a pre-set threshold; the tenants.segment sales label is not used to define it
Tenants: strata only; tenant fixed effects or stratified differences in the user-level analysis, tenant-clustered errors and size-weighting as robustness checks, and the sales segment label as a secondary comparison
