It doesn't break the plan, because the plan already avoids the problem. I define a 3P user by their own pre-test sessions, not by their tenant's '3P power' label. A user in a '3P power' tenant who never searches third-party content is classed as non-3P. A user in a 'standard' tenant who does search it is classed as 3P. If I had used the tenant label, the 3P group would have been diluted with users who never touch 3P content. That would pull the estimated effect toward zero and could wrongly argue against turning the panel on. The plan already says to report what share of '3P power' tenant users are 3P by behavior, and that number shows how large the mismatch is.
